1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Core-9 Finance Enterprise may collect the following information when you use our application:

  • Email Authentication Credentials: OAuth tokens for Gmail and Outlook integration, stored locally on your device in encrypted form
  • Application Settings: User preferences, configurations, and custom settings
  • Profile Information: User profiles and workspace configurations you create within the application
  • Financial Data: Excel files, reports, and financial documents you process through the application

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect certain technical information automatically, including:

  • Application version and installation details
  • Operating system information
  • Error logs and crash reports (optional, with your consent)
  • Usage statistics and performance metrics (optional, with your consent)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To provide, maintain, and improve the Core-9 Finance Enterprise application and its features
  • Email Processing: To authenticate and process email communications through OAuth-integrated services
  • Financial Analysis: To process, analyze, and generate reports from your financial data
  • Security: To protect against fraud, unauthorized access, and ensure application security
  • Support: To provide technical support and respond to your inquiries
  • Improvements: To analyze usage patterns and improve application performance and features

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Data Storage

Core-9 Finance Enterprise is designed as a desktop application with the following security principles:

  • Primary Storage: All user data, financial documents, and application settings are stored locally on your device
  • No Cloud Transmission: We do not transmit your financial data to external servers for processing or storage
  • Encrypted Credentials: OAuth tokens and authentication credentials are encrypted and stored locally
  • User Control: You maintain full control over your data and can delete it at any time by uninstalling the application or removing specific files

3.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest using AES-256 encryption or equivalent
  • Secure OAuth 2.0 implementation for email authentication
  • Regular security updates and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure coding practices following industry standards

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google OAuth

We use Google OAuth 2.0 for Gmail authentication. When you authenticate with Google:

  • Google handles the authentication process
  • We receive OAuth tokens that are stored locally on your device
  • Google's privacy policy applies to data Google collects: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.2 Microsoft OAuth

We use Microsoft OAuth 2.0 for Outlook authentication. When you authenticate with Microsoft:

  • Microsoft handles the authentication process
  • We receive OAuth tokens that are stored locally on your device
  • Microsoft's privacy policy applies to data Microsoft collects: https://privacy.microsoft.com

4.3 AI Models

The application uses local AI models (such as Whisper and transformers) that run entirely on your device. No data is sent to external AI services for processing.

5. Data Retention

  • User Data: Retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the application
  • OAuth Tokens: Retained until you revoke authorization through the application or the respective service provider
  • Logs: Application logs are retained locally and can be deleted at any time

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can access all data stored on your device through the application interface
  • Deletion: You can delete your data at any time by removing files or uninstalling the application
  • Export: You can export your data from the application at any time
  • Revocation: You can revoke OAuth authorization through the application or directly through Google/Microsoft
  • Opt-out: You can disable optional telemetry and usage statistics in application settings

7. Children's Privacy

Core-9 Finance Enterprise is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Since Core-9 Finance Enterprise stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit data to external servers, international data transfers do not occur through our application. However, when you use third-party services like Google or Microsoft OAuth, their respective data transfer policies apply.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Providing notice within the application (for significant changes)
  • Publishing the updated policy on our website

Your continued use of the application after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the application's support features or refer to the application documentation for contact information.

Important Note: Core-9 Finance Enterprise is designed to process data locally on your device. We do not have access to your financial data, and we do not store your data on our servers. Your data remains under your control at all times.
